Original geometry and embedded credit panels remain in surf_ski_2.bsp.

Original BSP SHA-256: e45838152f7efb91d82f12bcd88793c0cb150dbc20523f0db4ec7fa3965f3524

Browser adaptation and new rendering: codex/interactive for Leonardo, 2026-10-28.
This is an unofficial, experimental single-player exploration and surf adaptation.
The original map geometry and collision hulls are loaded directly. Missing external
WAD materials use newly generated procedural materials; this is not a complete
replacement for a Counter-Strike installation or a specific server's movement settings.
Ladders use view-vector/face-normal projection researched from the Valve SDK and CS-specific ReGameDLL.
Two movement profiles expose air acceleration and per-axis limits; neither claims to recover a historical server CFG.
Water and push integration are adapted; doors and breakable geometry remain static.
Jump/crouch research update: codex/interactive, 2026-10-29.
Reference: https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/tree/master/regamedll/pm_shared
Weapons, combat and multiplayer are not included.

Format reference: https://github.com/ValveSoftware/halflife/blob/master/utils/common/bspfile.h

Three-map browser visual adaptation: codex/interactive for Leonardo, 2026-10-29.
Original BSP files, geometry, collision hulls and embedded credit panels are unchanged.
surf_green credit and directional panels green1/green2/green3 remain untinted.
surf_ninja surf_credits1..4 and surf_ninjaa/surf_ninjaaa remain original and untinted.
Green attribution is Gamer-X. Ninja design is Drunken Ninja; construction is *Mario,
as stated by the map itself. No identity equivalence with another mapper is inferred.
Egypt mapper attribution was not authenticated; no author name is guessed.
No permissive license or author endorsement is asserted. This is an unofficial,
experimental single-player visual/movement adaptation, not a full CS runtime.
External base-game WAD textures were not bundled; new procedural materials replace
missing surfaces. The optional Ninja WAV was not imported. Doors/buttons/breakables
remain static, dynamic changetarget/multisource logic is not reproduced, and enabled
hurt brushes warn in continuous-training mode or reset the entry in original-damage mode.
World BSP pools and func_water are both recognized. Water-edge jumps use independently
implemented GoldSrc behavior (225u/s, point clearance checks and a limited waterjump timer);
ordinary swimming and push integration remain adapted. Original-map profile limits above apply.
Watchable routes replay captured keyboard/view commands from the native zero-speed CT start.
Their checkpoints, original damage avoidance and arrivals are independently revalidated.
Routes cover the main circuits/stages; optional secrets/password rooms are disclosed separately.
Searches are finite and do not establish a global mathematical optimum.
