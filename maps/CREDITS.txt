Original geometry and embedded credit panels remain in surf_ski_2.bsp.

Browser adaptation and new rendering: codex/interactive for Leonardo, 2026-10-28.
This is an unofficial, experimental single-player exploration and surf adaptation.
The original map geometry and collision hulls are loaded directly. Missing external
WAD materials use newly generated procedural materials; this is not a complete
replacement for a Counter-Strike installation or a specific server's movement settings.
Water, ladders and push integration are adapted; doors and breakable geometry remain static.
Weapons, combat and multiplayer are not included.
